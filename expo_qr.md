### How "Shake to Locate" Works:
- Anytime you have the app open on your physical phone, give the device a brisk physical shake.
- The app provides immediate haptic feedback, displays a floating glass HUD banner at the top, acquires your live GPS coordinates, centers the map, and sets your starting location!
- You can also toggle this feature on or off under **Profile & preferences → Settings → Shake to use live location**.
